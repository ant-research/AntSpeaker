# MECT: Mixture of Experts with CNN-Transformer Network for Speaker verification

**Official repository for the paper "MECT: Mixture of Experts with CNN-Transformer Network for Speaker verification"**


## Overview

MECT is a novel architecture that combines the strengths of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Transformer networks for speaker verification tasks. The model leverages a mixture of experts approach to enhance performance and robustness in speaker recognition.
